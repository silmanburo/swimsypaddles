From the coach
"WHEN I TEACH BEGINNERS, THE FIRST THING I NEED TO SHOW THEM IS HOW WATER FEELS."

Good swimming is built on the ability to feel the water and push off it correctly. We usually use different resistance tools and specific exercises. I even tried practising the stroke with a kickboard in hand — because its density helps you feel the water pressure more clearly. But it wasn't very comfortable, and that's exactly how this solution was born.

Classic paddles give a thinner resistance feel. Swimsy's larger surface changes that — the resistance is stronger and more noticeable. This helps activate the triceps faster and accelerates the development of water feel in ways standard paddles simply can't match.

— Alena, Swimsy founder & swimming coach
